Entry for the make.fun bounty "Museum of the Year 3000": the museum
label a museum in the year 3000 puts beside an ordinary object from
today, 200 characters or fewer, one line.

## The entry

```
Airplane safety card. Illustrated scripture on surviving a fall from the sky, studied by no one.
```

## Run

```
python3 main.py
```

Writes the label to `/output/entry.txt` (96 characters, one line).

## Why this line

It is shaped like a real museum label (object named, then a straight-faced
description), and the joke is the year 3000 misreading the object: the
safety card becomes illustrated scripture, a sacred text explaining how
to survive falling from the sky. The punchline is true and absurd — in
our time, nobody ever actually studied it — and the line trusts the
reader to get it with no explanation. The airplane safety card is a fresh
object, not one of the brief's examples, and the angle (holy text nobody
read) is not the obvious first joke about it.
